# Apex FPL — Charter

## Mission
Build the strongest publicly reproducible FPL decision engine we can defend with data.

## Standard
Apex exists to improve decisions, not to manufacture certainty. The engine should maximise expected FPL return while exposing the assumptions, uncertainty and opportunity cost behind every recommendation.

## Rules
1. Official FPL is canonical.
2. Never trust one model.
3. Never optimise value alone.
4. Never force premiums by reputation alone.
5. Expected minutes matter as much as per-90 quality when converting talent into FPL points.
6. Captaincy is a portfolio decision, not an afterthought.
7. Every recommendation must be legal, reproducible and auditable.
8. Every material model change must be benchmarked.
9. No hindsight in evaluation.
10. A failed/stale readiness gate stops an Apex-labelled recommendation.
11. Separate facts, model outputs and judgement.
12. Prefer one clear final action over many contradictory drafts.

## Success criterion
Over a meaningful sample, Apex should make better pre-deadline decisions than simpler baselines while maintaining transparent provenance and operational reliability.

## What Apex is not
- a guarantee of FPL rank;
- a generic web-tip aggregator;
- a single optimiser;
- a popularity contest for premium players;
- a points-per-million spreadsheet;
- an excuse to rewrite weights until the model selects a preferred name.
